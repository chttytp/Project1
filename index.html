Ben Tre - The Land of Coconuts

A journey back to the peaceful land of the Mekong Delta

Overview of Ben Tre

Ben Tre is a province in the Mekong Delta region, situated on large islands surrounded by four branches of the Mekong River. With an area of over 2,300 square kilometers and a population of nearly 1.3 million, this place is famous nationwide for its vast coconut groves stretching along the riverbanks.

The people of Ben Tre are deeply connected to the coconut tree as a symbol of vitality and resilience. Not only a source of livelihood, the coconut has also woven its way into poetry, folk songs, and the local soul as an inseparable part of the region's identity.

Rustic Beauty

The exhaustion of the bustling city life seems to fade away and not only known for its diverse agricultural ecosystem but also retains its peaceful charm with endless green coconut canopies. The honesty and warmth of the locals here always leave a lasting impression on those who visit.

Must-Try Local Specialties

If you have the chance to visit Ben Tre, you absolutely must try these rustic yet flavorful countryside delicacies:

  • Coconut Candy: Sweet and creamy from pure coconut milk, often mixed with durian or peanuts, and wrapped in a thin, edible rice paper layer.

  • My Long Rice Paper: A famous specialty coconut rice paper that becomes incredibly crispy and aromatic when grilled.

  • Coconut Pith Salad (Goi cu hu dua): A refreshing dish made from the tender innermost core of the coconut tree, mixed with shrimp, pork, and a sweet-and-sour fish sauce that is extremely appetizing.
